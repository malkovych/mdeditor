Privacy Policy — MD Editor

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Summary

MD Editor does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. There are no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no account required.

Your documents

Everything you write in MD Editor stays on your Mac. Documents are opened and saved only where you choose. The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox and can access only the files you explicitly open or save.

Network access

MD Editor works fully offline. The only network activity the app can perform is downloading remote images that you reference in your own Markdown documents (for example an image linked via an https URL), so they can be shown in the preview and included in PDF export. No other requests are made, and nothing is ever sent anywhere.

Third parties

MD Editor contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
