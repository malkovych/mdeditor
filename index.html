Frequently Asked Questions

How do I export a document as PDF?

Click the Export as PDF button in the toolbar, or press ⇧⌘E. Choose a location — a paginated PDF with proper page breaks is created.

Which files can I open?

Markdown files (.md, .markdown, .mdown, .mkd) and plain text (.txt). Documents autosave and keep version history.

How do I switch view modes?

Use the toolbar switcher or ⌥⌘1 Editor, ⌥⌘2 Split, ⌥⌘3 Preview.

How do I enable dark mode?

Click the appearance button (moon icon) in the toolbar and choose Auto, Light or Dark. Auto follows your system setting.

Does MD Editor work offline?

Yes, completely. The internet connection is used only to display remote images referenced in your documents.
